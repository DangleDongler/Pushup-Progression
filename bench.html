Bench Press Coach

Stand your phone upright on the floor at the foot of the bench, facing the bench, so the whole bar is in the picture.

After you start the camera, tap the end of the bar (or a weight plate) on the screen. The app follows that spot to count your reps.

Your video stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
