What do these numbers mean?

Elbow: 180° = arm straight, 90° = bent at a right angle.

Shoulder drop: how far your shoulders have come down toward your hands since the top of the rep. 0% = top, 100% = shoulders at hand level.

Hand gap: distance between your wrists compared with your shoulders. 1.0× = same as your shoulders, 2.0× = twice as wide.

Hip line: 0 = body straight. Plus = hips sagging. Minus = hips pushed up high. Only used when the camera is at a slant or side.

Body tilt: 0° = flat. Plus = head higher than feet.

Blue dots = your left side, orange dots = your right side.
