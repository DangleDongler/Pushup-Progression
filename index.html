What do these numbers mean?

Elbow: 180° = arm straight, 90° = bent at a right angle.

Hand gap: distance between your wrists compared with your shoulders. 1.0× = hands exactly shoulder-width apart, 2.0× = twice as wide.

Hip line: 0 = body straight as a plank. Plus numbers = hips sagging down. Minus numbers = hips pushed up high.

Body tilt: 0° = flat. Plus = head higher than feet (incline). Minus = feet higher than head (decline).

Camera view: small number = camera sees your side, big number = camera faces your head. "Slant" is what we want.

Blue dots = your left side, orange dots = your right side.
